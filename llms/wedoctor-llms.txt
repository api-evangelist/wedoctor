# WeDoctor (微医)

> Hangzhou-based Chinese digital health platform, founded 2010 as Guahao.com (挂号网).
> Operates online hospital-appointment booking, internet hospitals and online consultation,
> e-prescription and pharmacy fulfilment, screening and diagnostics, chronic-disease
> management, medical-insurance settlement, and the WeDoctor Cloud platform sold to hospitals
> and regional health authorities.

IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: WeDoctor publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no
API reference, no OpenAPI/GraphQL/AsyncAPI contract, no SDK, no MCP server and no agent card.
Every service below is a human-facing web or mobile surface requiring an interactive login and,
for clinical services, a verified Chinese identity. Do not attempt programmatic integration —
hospital, insurer and pharmacy integrations are arranged through private commercial agreements
with WeDoctor, not through a self-serve developer program.
